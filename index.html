The Process

How an Appointment Works

I

Call, text or book

Tell us the document, the city and when you're free. You'll get a firm time and a firm price right away.

II

We come to you

Home, office, hospital or care facility. Leave the signature line blank until we're together.

III

Signed and sealed

Most appointments take fifteen minutes. Payment when we finish.

Before We Arrive

Have This Ready

  • A current government photo ID for every signer
  • The complete document, still unsigned
  • All signers present at the same time
  • Any witnesses your document requires
  • Names spelled exactly as they appear on the ID

An expired ID is the most common reason an appointment can't be completed. Worth checking twice.
